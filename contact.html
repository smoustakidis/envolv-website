FAQ

Before you write

What does a pilot involve? +

We start with one building or one product line, agree what to measure, connect your data and review the results together. Scope and duration are agreed for each project.

Do you join EU research consortia? +

Yes. We contribute AI, data and LCA expertise to Horizon Europe and similar programmes, as partner or technology provider.

Which systems do you integrate with? +

IoT gateways, building management systems, BIM/IFC models, weather and energy-grid data. For LCI-Pilot, technical documents in common formats.
