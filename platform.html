LCI-Pilot · Environmental impact

Life Cycle Inventory, drafted by AI and signed off by experts

Developed in AMALTEA with façade manufacturers, LCI-Pilot reads technical documents and drafts inventory and cost data aligned with ISO 14040/14044 and EN 15804.

  1. 01UploadDatasheets, bills of materials, supplier declarations
  2. 02ExtractAI drafts flows, quantities and costs with source links
  3. 03ReviewAn LCA expert checks, corrects and approves
  4. 04ExportInventory ready for LCA tools and EPD preparation
